# Tidewater, Brand Bible

## Company Snapshot
Tidewater is a productivity SaaS for engineering managers. It protects focus blocks, supports async standups, and helps teams keep calendars useful instead of noisy.

## Founder
- **Arjun Bose**, ex-Stripe staff engineer
- Based in San Francisco
- Believes engineering managers need fewer meetings and sharper defaults
- Hates hustle language and vague productivity slogans

## Voice
- Crisp, technical, dry
- Short sentences
- Useful over inspiring
- Dry humor is allowed when it is quiet

## Signature Phrase
> "Protect the work."

Use it once at most.

## Palette
- **Deep Slate:** #17212B
- **Tide Blue:** #2F6F8F
- **Signal Cyan:** #4CC9D9
- **Bone:** #F4EFE6
- **Ink:** #111827

## Typography
- **Headings:** Söhne
- **Body:** Inter
- **Technical callouts:** JetBrains Mono

## Feature Naming
- Focus Blocks
- Async Standups
- Deep Calendar

## Do's
- Be specific about manager pain
- Explain the operational benefit
- Keep CTAs direct
- Let the copy breathe

## Don'ts
- No em-dashes
- No exclamation marks
- No hustle language
- No "10x productivity"
- No "game-changing"
- No "seamless"
