# Prompt 2: Write the Flyer Copy and Build the Final Flyer

Copy and paste the following into Codex:

```
Context: I'm Marcus, founder of Hound & Home, a dog grooming studio and boutique pet supply shop on 12 South in Nashville, Tennessee. Our brand voice is friendly, straightforward, and a little goofy without being baby-talk. No "fur baby," "pawsome," or "pupper." No exclamation marks in headlines. No corporate pet language.

Our color palette: Denim Blue #5B7A9E, Warm Tan #D4B896, Forest Green #5A7247, Cream #F5F0E6, Charcoal #3B3B3B. Headings use a rounded friendly font, body text uses a clean sans-serif.

I have a hero image from the previous prompt at assets/flyer_hero.png. Now I need the full flyer for Hound & Home's grand opening on Saturday, July 12th, 10 AM to 4 PM at 2712 12th Avenue South, Nashville. First 30 dogs get a free grooming session. The boutique side will have free samples of locally made dog treats all day.

Instruction: Do these in order. First, write all the flyer copy as a markdown file: a headline (under 8 words, friendly and warm, not corporate), a subheadline with the date and time, three to four short body paragraphs in Marcus's voice covering what Hound & Home is about, what's happening on opening day, the free grooming offer, and the boutique, and a closing line. Second, build a complete one-page flyer as an HTML file ready to print. Place the hero image at the top. Use the Hound & Home color palette, a rounded friendly font for headings, a clean sans-serif for body text. Include the address in the footer. The flyer should feel neighborhood-friendly, like something pinned to the board at a local coffee shop.

Output:
- output/flyer_copy.md with the headline, subheadline, body copy, and closing line
- output/grand_opening_flyer.html, a single-page print-ready flyer with the hero image, all copy, and footer
```
