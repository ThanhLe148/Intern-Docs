Act as a senior medtech strategy consultant and electrophysiology market analyst. Prepare a detailed, data-driven SWOT analysis of Boston Scientific’s FARAPULSE pulsed field ablation (PFA) system in the current global competitive landscape.

Objective:
Develop an investment-grade / strategy-grade SWOT analysis that assesses FARAPULSE as a product platform and as a competitive franchise within atrial fibrillation ablation and adjacent electrophysiology markets.

Scope:
- Focus on FARAPULSE and its related ecosystem (e.g., FARAWAVE, FARAWAVE NAV, FARASTAR, FARADRIVE, mapping/workflow integration, and relevant pipeline extensions if supported by evidence).
- Compare FARAPULSE against major current and emerging competitors in PFA and adjacent AF ablation, including at minimum:
  - Medtronic PulseSelect
  - Medtronic Affera / Sphere-9 (if relevant to the same workflow or future overlap)
  - Johnson & Johnson / Biosense Webster Varipulse
  - Abbott Volt PFA
  - Kardium Globe system
  - Thermal alternatives only where strategically relevant (RF/cryo) as incumbent substitutes
- Cover the U.S., Europe, and major ex-U.S. markets if data is available.

Research standards:
1. Use only verifiable, up-to-date sources.
2. Prioritize the most recent information available, especially from:
   - FDA announcements
   - CE mark / regulatory updates
   - Company press releases
   - earnings calls and investor presentations
   - peer-reviewed studies
   - Heart Rhythm Society (HRS), AF Symposium, EHRA, and major conference data
   - trial registries
   - reputable medtech trade publications
   - major sell-side / industry analyses where accessible
3. Cite every material factual claim inline with source name and date.
4. If evidence is mixed, explicitly say so.
5. Do not hallucinate market share, pricing, adoption, clinical performance, or physician preference. If exact data is unavailable, say “not publicly disclosed” and provide the closest supported proxy.

Required analytical framework:

A. Executive summary
- Provide a concise 8–12 bullet executive summary.
- State the single most important bullish thesis and the single most important bear thesis for FARAPULSE.

B. Market context
- Explain the current state of the PFA market:
  - technology adoption trends
  - shift from RF/cryo to PFA
  - total addressable market and procedure growth (if source-supported)
  - importance of workflow, mapping integration, safety profile, procedure time, learning curve, and expansion into persistent AF / complex arrhythmias
- Summarize where FARAPULSE sits in the market today.

C. Product and clinical profile of FARAPULSE
- Summarize:
  - mechanism of action
  - key product components
  - approved indications by region
  - major clinical trial and registry evidence
  - efficacy, safety, durability, workflow, and operator-learning considerations
- Separate clearly:
  - randomized evidence
  - single-arm evidence
  - registry / real-world evidence
  - company-reported data vs independent publications

D. Competitor benchmarking table
Create a detailed comparison table with columns such as:
- Company
- System/platform
- Regulatory status by region
- Key indication(s)
- Clinical evidence strength
- Mapping integration
- Workflow/procedure claims
- Safety profile
- Efficacy/durability data
- Physician usability / learning curve signals
- Commercial scale / installed base / adoption signals
- Strategic advantages
- Strategic disadvantages

E. SWOT analysis (main deliverable)
For each quadrant, provide 6–10 substantive points backed by evidence.

1. Strengths
Examples to analyze:
- clinical efficacy and safety evidence
- first-mover or scale advantages
- procedural simplicity
- mapping optionality vs integration
- commercial momentum
- physician adoption
- brand / channel / installed-base leverage
- pipeline and indication expansion potential

2. Weaknesses
Examples to analyze:
- evidence gaps
- dependence on narrow indications or specific catheter configurations
- workflow limitations
- integration disadvantages
- durability concerns vs competitors
- training burden
- pricing/reimbursement pressure
- overreliance on company-sponsored data
- vulnerabilities in complex arrhythmia use cases

3. Opportunities
Examples to analyze:
- expansion in persistent AF
- first-line ablation use
- redo ablation
- posterior wall / adjunctive lesion sets
- atrial flutter / CTI / ventricular applications if evidence supports
- international expansion
- ambulatory / ASC migration
- premium pricing / mix-shift potential
- cross-sell with mapping or EP ecosystem assets
- head-to-head evidence generation opportunities

4. Threats
Examples to analyze:
- competitor product launches and next-gen platforms
- integrated mapping ecosystems from rivals
- adverse clinical readouts
- safety events or post-market issues
- physician switching costs
- procurement pressure from hospitals / IDNs
- reimbursement changes
- commoditization as PFA matures
- legal / regulatory scrutiny
- faster innovation cycles from competitors

F. Competitive position assessment
- Answer explicitly:
  1. Is FARAPULSE currently the clinical leader, commercial leader, both, or neither?
  2. How durable is its leadership over the next 2–3 years?
  3. Which competitor is the most credible threat and why?
  4. What are the top 3 factors that could improve or weaken FARAPULSE’s position materially?

G. Scenario analysis
Provide three scenarios:
- Base case
- Upside case
- Downside case

For each scenario include:
- assumptions
- likely market/competitive developments
- implications for FARAPULSE adoption, pricing power, and strategic position

H. Final judgment
- End with a sharp conclusion:
  - “Net strategic position: Very strong / strong / mixed / vulnerable”
  - Explain why in one paragraph.
- Then provide:
  - Top 5 watch items for the next 12 months
  - Top 5 diligence questions an investor, strategist, or medtech executive should ask

Output requirements:
- Use clear section headings
- Use bullet points plus narrative analysis
- Include at least one competitor comparison matrix
- Include one final SWOT summary table
- Include a source list grouped by:
  - regulatory
  - company disclosures
  - clinical literature
  - conference presentations
  - industry/market commentary

Quality bar:
- Be analytical, not descriptive.
- Distinguish evidence from inference.
- Highlight where Boston Scientific messaging may be promotional versus independently validated.
- Where available, compare FARAPULSE not just on efficacy/safety but also on usability, integration, market access, and strategic monetization.
- If a key datapoint cannot be verified, label it explicitly as unknown rather than estimating.

Tone:
Professional, rigorous, data-driven, investor-grade, and suitable for senior leadership, private equity diligence, equity research, or strategic planning.
