# Prompt 1: Generate the Flyer Hero Image

Copy and paste the following into Codex:

```
Context: I'm Maya, founder of Bloom & Butter, a small-batch bakery and flower bar on SE Division Street in Portland, Oregon. We opened in 2024. We're known for seasonal pastries, naturally leavened sourdough, and small floral arrangements you can grab with your morning coffee. Everything is baked fresh daily. Our brand voice is warm, unhurried, and slightly playful but never cutesy. It reads like a handwritten note on the counter, not a marketing email. We never use corporate food language like "flavor profile" or "curated selection," no ALL CAPS, no emoji, and no urgency marketing.

Our color palette: Warm Cream #FAF3E8, Soft Terracotta #C4836A, Sage Green #8B9E7E, Charcoal #3A3632, Butter Yellow #E8C96E. Headings use Recoleta (rounded serif), body text uses DM Sans.

We're opening in two weeks and I need a grand opening flyer I can print and post around the neighborhood. First I need a beautiful hero image I can use at the top of the flyer.

Instruction: Generate one hero image for the flyer. The image should show a warm, inviting bakery counter scene with fresh sourdough loaves, a small bouquet of seasonal flowers, and soft morning light coming through a window. Use the Bloom & Butter color palette. The style should feel hand-illustrated and textured, not photorealistic. Think watercolor meets linocut.

Output:
- assets/flyer_hero.png, a warm on-brand hero image for the grand opening flyer
```
