# Lumen Pilates, Brand Bible

## Company Snapshot
Lumen Pilates is a boutique reformer pilates studio in Austin, Texas, founded in 2023 by Sofia Martinez. Two studios, one in South Congress and one in East Austin. Known for small classes (max 8 reformers), a strong member community, and a no-hustle approach to fitness.

## Founder
- **Sofia Martinez**, 38, former dancer and physical therapist
- Lives in East Austin
- Believes movement should feel good, not punishing
- Hates fitness shame culture

## Locations
- **South Congress** (flagship, 2023)
- **East Austin** (opened 2024)

## Mission
Help people feel stronger and more connected to their bodies, in classes small enough that the instructor knows your name by week two.

## Voice and Tone
- Warm, grounded, slightly poetic but never floaty
- Encouraging without being motivational poster cliches
- Uses body-positive language without sounding performative
- Calm pacing, short sentences

## Sample Voice
**Yes:**
> "This week we're slowing down. Longer holds, fewer reps. Pay attention to where you carry your tension."

**No:**
> "Crush your goals this week with our HIIT-fused power pilates fusion! No excuses!"

## Class Naming Convention
Classes use sensory or natural language, not intensity language. Examples:
- "Slow Roll" (restorative)
- "Long Lines" (full body strength)
- "Spine Day" (back focus)
- "Open Hips" (mobility)
- "Reset" (post-injury and beginners)

## Customer Persona
- Women 28 to 50 who have tried Orangetheory or F45 and bounced off
- Pregnant and postpartum women
- People returning to fitness after injury
- Members value community as much as the workout

## Do's
- Reference the body honestly: tension, breath, alignment, mobility
- Use seasonal language (the way bodies feel in summer vs winter)
- Speak to where members actually are in life
- Name instructors when you mention them

## Don'ts
- No "boss babe" or hustle language
- No before/after weight loss photos
- No "transform your body" or "get summer ready"
- No counting calories or punishing language
- No emoji-heavy posts

## Reusable Phrases
- "Stronger. Slower. On purpose."
- "Eight reformers. One room. The work happens here."
- "Small classes. Real attention."
